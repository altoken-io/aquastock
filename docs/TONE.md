# AquaStock tone and UI-writing guide

## Voice

AquaStock sounds clear, civic, and concrete — the confidence of infrastructure that's actually accountable, not a startup pitch. It should read as trustworthy public-interest tooling, not a token launch.

The desired feeling is **capital you can see land**: a government commitment and a community contribution, both visible, both tracked against the same milestones. Write as a transparency tool, not a crypto protocol or an investment platform.

| Use                                  | Avoid                                      |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| "Government contribution: confirmed" | "Institutional capital deployed on-chain"  |
| "3 of 5 milestones verified"         | "Trustless milestone attestation achieved" |
| "Fund this project"                  | "Ape into this opportunity"                |
| "See the transaction"                | "Immutable proof of settlement"            |
| "This is a hackathon demo"           | anything implying a real, live offering    |

## Tone by moment

- **Project/funding actions:** short, direct. "Fund this position." "View on Explorer." "Milestone verified."
- **Public vs. private framing:** precise, never editorializing — state who funded what and when, let the split speak for itself rather than dramatizing "public vs. private."
- **Milestones and impact:** factual, dated, specific ("households connected," not "lives transformed").
- **Legal/disclosures:** always the demo disclaimer, never diluted with marketing language.

## Point of view and language

Use second person for actions: "Connect your wallet," "Fund this project." Use third person for product definition: "AquaStock tracks…"

Write in plain English or clear Peruvian Spanish per the active locale — translate meaning, not word order. Keep product names and technical terms unchanged: **AquaStock**, **Solana**, **Solana Pay**. Never expose implementation details (enum values like `PUBLIC`/`PRIVATE`, database IDs, internal status strings) in user-facing copy.
